# Signal Boost

The Signal Boost tactic is a simple but effective way to increase the visibility of your replies with the original author. By liking a post when you reply to it, you trigger a notification that signals engagement and increases the likelihood of the author seeing and interacting with your reply.

## Why It Works

When you like a tweet after replying to it, the author receives a second notification — first for the reply, then for the like. This double-signal increases the chance they'll click through and engage with your comment. The author sees that you didn't just reply, but also appreciated their content enough to like it.

This works because:

- **Double notification:** The author sees your name twice in their notifications, creating familiarity
- **Social proof:** A like on your own reply signals to the author that the interaction was valuable
- **Algorithmic favorability:** The X algorithm weights replies with subsequent likes more positively
