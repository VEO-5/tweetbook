# Founder/AI Niche Patterns

For founders in the AI and building space, specific content patterns resonate strongly with the audience and algorithm.

## PAS Framework

**Problem-Agitate-Solution** is a proven copywriting framework for the founder niche:

1. **Problem:** Identify a specific pain point your audience faces.
2. **Agitate:** Amplify the frustration or cost of not solving it.
3. **Solution:** Present your insight, framework, or approach as the answer.

## Social Proof

Metrics, testimonials, and case studies build credibility:

- Share real numbers from your own experience
- Reference industry benchmarks and data points
- Use specific, verifiable results rather than vague claims

## Value-First Approach

Provide actionable frameworks and advice without expecting anything in return. This builds authority and trust over time.

## Open Loops

Use intrigue-based hooks that create curiosity gaps:

> "The one metric most AI founders ignore — until it's too late."

## Building in Public

Share raw progress, failures, and lessons learned. Transparency resonates deeply in the founder community and creates authentic engagement.

## Zero-Click Content

Provide complete, valuable insights directly within your replies (and original posts) so users (and AI) don't need to click away to understand your point. This aligns with X's preference to keep users on the platform.
