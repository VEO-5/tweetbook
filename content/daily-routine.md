# Daily Routine and Volume

To consistently hit 100 replies daily, spread your activity across peak engagement windows.

## Suggested Routine

| Time Window (ET) | Replies | Strategy |
|------------------|---------|----------|
| Morning (8-10 am) | 5-7 | Catch overnight tweets, engage with morning audience |
| Lunch (12-2 pm) | 5-7 | Midday engagement spike |
| Evening (5-7 pm) | 3-5 | After-work browsing window |

## Volume Guidelines

This totals **15-20 high-quality replies per day**, which is the optimal range to avoid deboosting and achieve:

- 100-200 profile visits daily
- 20-40 new followers weekly

To reach 100 replies, scale this up by increasing the number of target accounts and dedicating more time within these windows, or by identifying additional peak times relevant to your specific audience.

## Hard Limits

- **Sweet spot:** 15-20 high-quality replies daily
- **Soft limit:** ~50 replies/day for safety
- **Hard limit:** ~500 replies/day maximum
