# Getting Started

This guide outlines an optimal Twitter (now X) reply strategy designed for individuals in the founder, AI, and building niche. The objective is to significantly enhance presence and engagement through strategic replies.

## Overview

The "Reply Guy" strategy, when executed correctly, is a powerful method for growth on X. By focusing on high-quality, value-adding replies to strategic targets, you can dramatically increase your visibility and follower count.

## The Core Philosophy

Success on X in 2026 is built on three pillars:

1. **Conversation Quality:** The algorithm now weights replies 27x more than likes. Genuine, thoughtful conversation drives growth.
2. **Strategic Targeting:** Not all engagements are equal. Focus on accounts with 5-20x your follower count within your niche.
3. **Consistent Volume:** 15-20 high-quality replies daily produces compounding growth over time.

## Quick Start Checklist

- [ ] Optimize your profile with niche keywords
- [ ] Identify 20-30 target accounts in your niche
- [ ] Set up a daily routine (morning, lunch, evening)
- [ ] Master the 7 reply types
- [ ] Track your metrics weekly
- [ ] Adjust based on what works

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Target | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Daily replies | 15-20 | Optimal for growth without deboosting |
| Profile visits | 100-200/day | Indicator of reply visibility |
| New followers | 20-40/week | Conversion from profile visits |
| Reply engagement rate | >5% | Signal of quality responses |
